# Basis for this implementation

Project inputs supplied by the owner:

- `CounterEx_Roadmap.pptx`: slides 3–6 define the research questions, feedback loop,
  divisible-pair and derangement examples, and planned evaluations. Slides 7–9
  outline the schedule, risks and early engineering work.
- `CounterEx_Literature_Survey.xlsx`: includes 51 paper entries plus learning
  resources and datasets/tools. Its guide explicitly distinguishes previously
  checked links from identifiers recalled from memory.

The implementation follows the two concrete examples and counterexample-feedback
mechanism. It uses original synthetic fixtures and reviewed oracles. It does not
bundle benchmark data, claim literature novelty, reproduce reported paper results,
or claim to have read the linked papers. Bibliographic links and quantitative
claims in the supplied files have not been independently verified in this milestone.

Before writing the related-work chapter, verify the primary sources for the
survey's highlighted overlapping work: HardTests, AutoCode, CodeHacker and DePro.
Record the exact methods, datasets, model sizes and differences from CounterEx.
The research contribution must come from the later controlled evaluation, not
from implementing a stress-test loop alone.
